House rules
  • Both back rows start as pawns, apart from the Kings on e1 and e8.
  • A capture earns 2 points, whatever was taken (en passant included). A turn without a capture earns 1 point, including a turn ended by a checking upgrade. Points arrive at the end of your turn.
  • Before moving, you may spend points to upgrade one piece. The price is the difference in value: Pawn 1, Knight 3, Bishop 3, Rook 5, Queen 9.
  • Knights and Bishops can't be swapped for each other. Kings and Queens can't be upgraded.
  • If an upgrade gives check, your turn ends there with no move. That isn't allowed while your own King is in check.
  • An upgrade is only allowed if you'll still have a legal move afterwards (or it gives check).
  • Only pawns on the second row may move two squares. Reaching the far row promotes for free, as usual.
  • Castling needs an unmoved King and an unmoved Rook in the corner. A corner pawn upgraded to a Rook without moving counts.
  • If you have no legal move and no legal upgrade, it's checkmate when in check, otherwise stalemate.
